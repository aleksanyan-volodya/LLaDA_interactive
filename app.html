How it works
1Start from noiseThe answer begins fully masked. Only the prompt is given.
2Predict every mask at onceA bidirectional transformer guesses all masked tokens in parallel, using context on both sides.
3Keep a few, remask the restThe most confident (or random) guesses are kept. The others are masked again and predicted at the next step.
4Repeat until nothing is maskedFewer steps is faster, but tokens revealed together cannot see each other: quality drops.
Re-implementation at small scale of Large Language Diffusion Models (Nie et al., 2025) · trained from scratch on TinyStories
